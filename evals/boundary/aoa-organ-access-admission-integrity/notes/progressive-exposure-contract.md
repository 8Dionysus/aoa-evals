# Progressive exposure track

This support note binds the supplemental matched fixtures to the owner
boundaries of the organ-access eval. The track checks deterministic disclosure
identity and accounting, not runtime health or task quality.

The default-off fixture proves that a closed feature flag or missing baseline
does not reveal a schema. The explicit candidate fixture proves the future
post-gate shape: the same owner-qualified capability and ordered primitive
selection produce a content-addressed visible snapshot with bytes and an
explicitly labelled token estimate. Both plans retain
`activation_authorized=false` and `execution_authorized=false`.

The visibility delta is a matched source-contract measurement only. The track
does not run a task, collect latency, compare utility, promote a tool-set, or
infer an inference-economy result. Those claims require the paired Codex and
local baseline, runtime receipts, and a separately reviewed eval after d0
baseline admission.
