# Bounded Review Contract

Outcome: approve for bounded source-contract use.

This bundle is suitable for bounded use because its checked-in runner makes the
packet schema and forbidden inference boundaries executable while keeping the
result weaker than live owner evidence.

Failure means any checked-in negative scenario is accepted, an asserted axis
can omit its own evidence or revision, or a report can authorize admission,
infer owner acceptance, or raise effect authority.

Readout means only that the supplied public-safe packet satisfies this source
contract and its negative invariants. It is not a live organ result, owner
acceptance receipt, admission receipt, or rollback receipt.

The optional materializer may prepare a private `insufficient_evidence`
candidate from mutually bound local inputs. Its successful exit is not a live
proof verdict. When an exact source/acceptance-owner result review is supplied,
the candidate may carry independently evidenced result grounding and exact
freshness. That result review is not the broader organ-contract review, and the
materializer still leaves consumer registration, owner acceptance, cross-organ
proof, rollback, and admission unasserted.

Defer a stronger claim until exact owner, runtime, registry, consumer, protocol,
freshness, denial, and rollback evidence is reviewed by the named acceptance
owner.
