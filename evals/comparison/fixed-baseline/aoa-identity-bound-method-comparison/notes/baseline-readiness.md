# Baseline readiness

The bundle is a draft design and is not a current real-session baseline.

Readiness for a future owner-local apply requires:

- a complete `exact_fit` apply packet;
- a source or candidate digest and explicit environment identity;
- all nine identity fields on every baseline and method row;
- known cache and resource posture for any observed pair;
- reviewed or controlled evidence, with provisional rows retained as unmatched;
- explicit first-failure and retry fields, even when their state is null or
  unobservable;
- a fresh, reviewed cohort rather than a generated index or delivery receipt.

Until those conditions hold, the only honest report is `not_admitted` or a
controlled accounting disposition. A generated reader or green validator is
not baseline evidence.
