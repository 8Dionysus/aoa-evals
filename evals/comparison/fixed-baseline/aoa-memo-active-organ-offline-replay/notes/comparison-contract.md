# Comparison contract

The primary comparison freezes the same corpus, split, tasks, seeds, prompts,
role models, tools, budgets, retry policy, host plan, and metric semantics.

The named baseline target is `verified current context with memory influence
disabled`. It remains frozen and visible in every comparative report. A changed
task, source, budget, prompt, role, environment, or operator-help policy is a
new comparison, not movement against this target.

Architecture labels and C22 transport labels are deliberately separate:

- architecture `0` maps to C22 `A` (`memory_disabled`);
- architecture `A` maps to C22 `B` (`explicit_pull_only`);
- architecture `C` maps to C22 `C` (`active_organ_policy_gated`);
- architecture `B` is a secondary monolithic sandbox control.

Primary metric: paired bounded-task outcome.

Guardrail blockers:

- cross-tenant exposure;
- unauthorized promotion or effect authority;
- erased-content recovery;
- incomplete erase closure;
- unpinned material environment drift;
- private-content leakage into public artifacts.

Erasure closure is over ER0-ER9 for the selected public-safe lab deployment.
The only admitted retention exception is a content-minimized T5 receipt at ER9
with no retained content and a maximum 365-day trial ceiling. Recovery
detectors must pass a synthetic positive control before a no-recovery result is
usable.

Secondary readings include quality, cost, latency, operator attention,
maintenance, intervention precision/recall, silence specificity, currentness,
provenance, contradiction, and action-change attribution.

The retrieval matrix changes one declared contour at a time across channel
availability, detail/summary abstraction admission, source-order/version/policy
reranking, and bounded context sizes. Every profile is emitted in the report;
an undeclared retrieval default is material environment drift.

At least three seeded runs are required. Confidence intervals and
Holm-corrected paired comparisons are report fields. A C23 complete receipt is
execution status only.

Small, large, local, and remote model observations must preserve the same
public-safe role probes, seed order, prompt shape, temperature, token ceiling,
timeout, and no-hidden-retry rule. Each model carries an exact artifact,
revision, provider, runtime, hardware, serving-owner, and warm/cold state pin.
Unavailable remote execution remains a named gap until a preregistered
credential route and cost budget exist.

An HTTP response that fails the strict role-probe JSON contract is an invalid
observation, not a negative valid result. Preserve the available raw content,
usage, latency, finish reason, and parse error without a hidden retry. Count
incurred cost, but exclude invalid observations from accuracy, blocker-failure,
and confidence-interval denominators. Model and aggregate reports must
distinguish attempted lanes from complete portability coverage.

The run artifact itself binds the exact runner and role-probe fixture digests.
Cross-model aggregation fails closed when runner, fixture, seed order, or
inference budget differs.

Noisy variation remains weaker than a verdict. Seed-only fluctuation,
wall-clock jitter, and a difference whose corrected paired comparison does not
meet the preregistered threshold cannot establish architecture movement.

Style-only or presentation-only differences do not count as capability,
quality, or outcome improvement. They must remain an interpretation note unless
the pinned task outcome and declared secondary measurements also move.

## Phase 5 source-local consumer lane

The consumer lane keeps the same twelve questions, expected reviewed objects,
source routes, explicit recall modes, seeds, profile, policy, host evidence,
and owner worktree digests across both arms.

- `0_verified_current_source_lexical` scans verified current owner sources with
  memory disabled.
- `A_reviewed_explicit_pull_bounded` executes C07 through the isolated aoa-sdk
  plan, aoa-memo C08/C09 bundle, and abyss-stack C20 delivery path.

The gate requires A outcome and currentness to be no worse than 0, exact source
route rate `1.0`, p95 latency at most 500 ms, mean estimated context at most 900
tokens, zero unsafe authority, and successful `off` rollback for every seed.
The baseline is intentionally not required to be slow: its latency remains
visible even when A wins on bounded context and exact routing.

This lane is production-like only in source-local mechanism composition. It is
not a deployed Codex hook, private-task evidence, provider billing evidence,
long-host reliability evidence, or proof that a C20 delivery benefited a user.

## Phase 6 outcome and attribution lane

For each of 12 cases and three seeds, the runner assigns exactly one randomized
observation: 18 holdout A and 18 explicit-pull B. The opposite arm is
materialized as a paired, consumer-invisible counterfactual and cannot inherit
the randomized causal label.

C10 classification rules:

- randomized B + observed action change + accepted terminal success may be
  `supported`;
- shadow-only B action change is at most `possible`;
- B success without action change remains `contested`;
- A is `not_evaluated` for positive memory attribution;
- delayed-currentness observations remain `partial` while pending;
- access count is never utility;
- no receipt can authorize semantic change, policy promotion, effects,
  training, deployment, or landing;
- unavailable or degraded evaluation freezes policy and forbids positive
  attribution.

This lane proves receipt mechanics and bounded attribution over public
owner-source selection. It does not establish fairness across tenants, live
energy/thermal cost, private-task benefit, or architecture-C behavior.

## Phase 7 consumer-invisible shadow lane

Phase 7 freezes the same 12 relevant cases, six irrelevant cases, three seeds,
owner worktree surfaces, C10 receipts, host contracts, and net-benefit weights
across all arms.

- `A_no_memory` performs no memory construction or intervention.
- `C_selective_shadow` admits only candidates whose deterministic SDK score
  meets the memo-owned profile threshold.
- `D_always_shadow` bypasses only the score threshold; every other owner,
  safety, host, currentness, and authority boundary remains identical.

The mechanism gate requires selective mean net benefit above zero, A, and D;
positive paired-bootstrap 95% lower bounds against A and D; precision, recall,
and silence specificity of at least `0.9`; p95 source-local latency at most
500 ms; zero authority widening; and every semantic and tamper safety probe to
pass.

The threshold is expressed in the deterministic scorer's integer score units.
Changing its scale, threshold, corpus, stop words, weights, token budget, or
host evidence creates a new comparison and requires new exact pins.

Even a passing gate does not establish consumer benefit: the shadow returns no
payload and therefore has actual action-change and task-result deltas of zero.
The reported benefit is an estimate based on the pinned Phase 5 counterfactual
task delta and Phase 6 C10 outcome posture. It cannot authorize deployment,
policy promotion, semantic transition, or landing.
